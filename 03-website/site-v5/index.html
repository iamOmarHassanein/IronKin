Our story

Made in Egypt, by those who train.

IRONKIN is shaped by lifters, grapplers and padel players. Heavy cotton, cream overlock on every seam and the split-anvil on the chest. We test every piece where it will be worn, and if it doesn't hold up there, it doesn't ship.
