About IRONKIN

IRONKIN is a temple for those who honour the ritual of training: the weight of the bar, the discipline of the mat, the precision of the court. Every piece is a vow, sealed in burgundy and cream.

Rite I · Strength

Under the bar there is no audience. Only the weight, the breath, and the piece you trust to move with you. Signature Hoodie · Wide-Leg Pant.

Rite II · Jiu-Jitsu

On the mat, the kit is a second skin. Cream overlock that holds through every scramble, every grip, every roll. Compression Tee.

Rite III · Padel

On the court, precision is a form of devotion. A fitted line that follows the swing and never fights it. Fitted Tee · Flared Pant.
