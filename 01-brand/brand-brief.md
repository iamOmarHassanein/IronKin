## Identity

Approved direction: [identity/source/direction-board.webp](identity/source/direction-board.webp). Boards and logo files: [identity/](identity/).

- **Tagline:** Forged together.
- **Voice:** TBD. The tagline and boards suggest blunt, short, crew-first lines ("You're one of us.", "Built in the dark."), but that is a draft, not a decision.
- **Logo:** split anvil symbol (two halves joined by a crack), IRONKIN wordmark, arched wordmark for garment backs, and a stacked lockup with the tagline. A distressed print finish is for campaigns and garments only. Files: `identity/logo/svg/` and `identity/logo/png/`.
- **Colors:** Forge Black `#0B0B0A` (about 60% of a layout), Bone `#EEE7DC` (25%), Oxblood `#8A1019` (10%, accents only), Ash `#2A2A28` (5%, panels and garments).
- **Typography:** Barlow Condensed ExtraBold for headlines, Barlow Condensed Medium tracked wide for labels, Barlow Regular for body. The wordmark is drawn artwork and is never retyped in a font.
- **Rules:** see [identity/boards/04-logo-rules.png](identity/boards/04-logo-rules.png). No stretching, recoloring, outlining or rotating. Clear space equals the anvil foot height.
